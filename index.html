v1.5.1

03 OCT 2026 - GO:COUNTER

Hello again!

Yes, updates are coming in left in right but apparently you people like it so here are the patch notes!

Update Size: 1.57MB

Download Link

Note: As long you don’t have the updated build, you will not be able to play on our servers


Operation Wildfire

– Mission drops now show up properly. Items you earn from completing a mission now appear in the end-of-match drop list and are marked as a mission drop.
– Mission drops now come from every collection. Instead of mostly Dragon Lores and Medusas, a mission drop now picks a random collection and rolls an item from it, using the real CS:GO odds.
– You voted, we listened: the rarer tiers are 2x as likely as Valve’s original odds. A Covert from the Cobblestone collection (yes, the Dragon Lore) is now a 0.0512% chance instead of 0.0256% for example.
– Every first completion of a mission now rewards a drop.

Souvenirs

– Fixed souvenir items not having any stickers.
– Events from before autographs existed get an extra event sticker in place of the autograph. All stickers always come from the souvenir’s own event.

Music Kits

– Music kits can now drop at the end of a match again. Roughly 1 in every 3–4 matches.
– Every music kit that drops is StatTrak and counts your MVPs from now on.
– All music kits already in your inventory have been upgraded to StatTrak.

Store

– Case prices are now between 2.99 and 4.99 fMt$, and the shop still rotates every hour (Have fun you gamblers).

Fixes

– Fixed some stickers and items not being recognized by the game coordinator (for example the MLG Columbus 2016 stickers).
– Fixed items showing the wrong origin (for example dropped items showing up as purchased).

Known Issues

– Season collection not showing up ingame
– Typo STILL in the Noscope message (I am lazy okay?..)

v1.5 - Inventory Rework and GO:COUNTER Store

01 OCT 2026 - GO:COUNTER

Hello everyone!

The GO:COUNTER Store is finally live, inventories got a proper overhaul, and we squashed a bunch of bugs along the way.

Update Size: 1.55 MB

Note: As long you don’t have the updated build, you will not be able to play on our servers


Patch Notes:

GO:COUNTER Store

– The Store is now live: buy cases with your balance (fMt$)
– The shop rotates every hour with 6 cases
– You can buy up to 20 cases at once
– Removed Case refill from the market website

Matchmaking

– The main menu now shows real stats (players online, servers, ongoing matches and search time)
– A player joining or leaving your party now stops the search and tells you who it was
– Only the party leader can cancel the search

Inventory

– Inventory GC system has been completely remade from scratch and is not plugin dependant anymore
– Match drops work again, and **all** drops are shown now
– Unboxes, trade-ups and trades are now announced in chat properly
– Website trades and market purchases now show up in-game instantly
– Offline games with bots now show your skins and loadout changes

Trade-Ups

– Results now only come from your input collections, with the original odds and floats

Settings

– Added new options to the Game Settings and Video Settings menus

Bugs

– Fixed the server hanging when many players joined at once
– Fixed a crash when joining a server
– Fixed some issues with Wildfire Progression
– Fixed a issue where you could trade up a knife from pink items

Known Bugs

– Noscope Chat message prints on the victim a extra x behind their name due to a typo
– Buying something from the store can cause your game to crash sometimes

v1.4.1

26 SEP 2026 - GO:COUNTER

Hello everyone!

Thanks a lot for all the bug reports since yesterday’s big update!
We went through them and are now releasing a hotfix that fixes a lot of them, plus a few new things on top.

Update Size: 1.2MB (yes, that small!)

Note: You need the new client v1.4.1 from our Discord. Older clients will show an “out of date” notice now in the main menu and can’t join matchmaking anymore.

Patch Notes:
– Added the Service Medal functionality in the main menu
– Added an update check that tells you when your client is out of date
– Added a “Remove sticker?” confirmation before a fully worn sticker gets removed
– Changed the Steam Profile button on the scoreboard to open the player’s profile
– Changed the Community Market button in the inventory to open the market
– Fixed not being able to equip the default M4A1-S, USP-S, CZ75-Auto and R8 Revolver without owning a skin
– Fixed switching back to the M4A4, P2000 or Desert Eagle not replacing the equipped weapon
– Fixed the inventory not updating until you reopened it (stickers, name tags and more)
– Fixed Name Tags disappearing from the inventory after using them
– Fixed older skins (including Consumer and Industrial grade) not being usable in trade-ups
– Fixed the trade-up reveal animation not playing
– Fixed sticker scraping not working and ending in an error
– Fixed weapons not showing up as a new item after applying or removing a sticker
– Fixed stickers not appearing ingame due to a plugin bug

Keep the bug reports coming and good luck to everyone playing in fragFall!

-NeXi2k
