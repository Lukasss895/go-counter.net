v1.5.1

03 Oct 2026

Hello again!

Yes, updates are coming in left in right but apparently you people like it so here are the patch notes!

Update Size: 1.57MB

Download Link

Note: As long you don’t have the updated build, you will not be able to play on our servers


Operation Wildfire

– Mission drops now show up properly. Items you earn from completing a mission now appear in the end-of-match drop list and are marked as a mission drop.
– Mission drops now come from every collection. Instead of mostly Dragon Lores and Medusas, a mission drop now picks a random collection and rolls an item from it, using the real CS:GO odds.
– You voted, we listened: the rarer tiers are 2x as likely as Valve’s original odds. A Covert from the Cobblestone collection (yes, the Dragon Lore) is now a 0.0512% chance instead of 0.0256% for example.
– Every first completion of a mission now rewards a drop.

Souvenirs

– Fixed souvenir items not having any stickers.
– Events from before autographs existed get an extra event sticker in place of the autograph. All stickers always come from the souvenir’s own event.

Music Kits

– Music kits can now drop at the end of a match again. Roughly 1 in every 3–4 matches.
– Every music kit that drops is StatTrak and counts your MVPs from now on.
– All music kits already in your inventory have been upgraded to StatTrak.

Store

– Case prices are now between 2.99 and 4.99 fMt$, and the shop still rotates every hour (Have fun you gamblers).

Fixes

– Fixed some stickers and items not being recognized by the game coordinator (for example the MLG Columbus 2016 stickers).
– Fixed items showing the wrong origin (for example dropped items showing up as purchased).
