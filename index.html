v1.0 - GO:COUNTER 2016 Era Begins!

20 OCT 2026 - GO:COUNTER

Welcome to the beginning of the GO:COUNTER project!

Launcher & Main Menu

– Custom modified launcher optimized for a smoother and faster gaming experience.
– Cleaned up main menu: simplified from 5 tabs to 3 essential ones (PLAY, INVENTORY, OPTIONS).
– Working "Play with Friends" lobby: invite and play together with your friends!

Inventory & Upcoming Events

– Inventory system is currently in development and is planned to release around November or December.
– Festive update planned for December with custom Christmas-themed maps!

Game Modes

– Wingman: Our official custom mode inspired by 2018 Wingman, updated with a fresh map pool.
– Retakes: Powered by splewis’ popular plugin from GitHub.

Custom Matchmaking Browser

– Built our own Play Matchmaking system! You can access it directly in-game via the main menu browser. Simply pick your mode, wait for a match, hit Accept, and connect via the IP address provided in your console.

Enjoy this fresh start and thank you for being part of the GO:COUNTER community!
